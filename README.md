# ph.ink

A free, open-source link shortener with built-in click analytics and QR codes.

**Status:** early development. Nothing is deployed yet.

## Goals

- Unlimited short links and QR codes, free
- Analytics without tracking cookies or stored IP addresses
- Editable QR codes, plus a static QR option that works without ph.ink
- Safe by design: abuse protection built in
