# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security problem in phub.ink, please tell us privately first.

- Email **phoniqueshub@gmail.com** with the subject "Security report".
- Or use GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" button on the repository's Security tab, if it is shown.

Please do not open a public issue or post details until we have had a chance to fix it.

Include what you found, the address or page affected, and the steps to reproduce it. We aim to reply within five working days and to keep you updated until it is fixed. We are happy to credit you if you want that.

## Scope

In scope: phub.ink and the code in this repository.

Out of scope: denial-of-service or load testing, spam or social engineering of our staff, findings that need a rooted or already-compromised device, and reports from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact.

## Please

- Only test against your own account and your own links.
- Do not access, change or delete other people's data.
- Stop and tell us if you reach someone else's data by accident.

## Supported versions

Only the version running at phub.ink is supported.
