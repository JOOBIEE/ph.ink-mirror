Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. This page says what phub.ink stores, why, how long we keep it, and how you can take it with you or delete it.

Who runs this

phub.ink is run by PhoniquesHub in Lagos, Nigeria. For privacy questions or requests, write to phoniqueshub@gmail.com or omotoshoolayemi92@gmail.com.

What we store

If you have an account:

  • Your email address, and when you created your account.
  • Your links: the short ending, the destination, and the name you gave it.
  • The colours and shapes of QR codes you saved for a link. Logos you add in the QR studio stay in your browser and are never uploaded.
  • A hashed copy of your sign-in link and of your session, never the raw values. There are no passwords.

When someone opens a short link or scans its QR code, we record:

  • The time, the country, the device type, the browser, and the website they came from (only the site's name).
  • Whether it was a link click or a QR scan, and whether it looked like a bot.
  • A scrambled visitor fingerprint made from the visitor's network address and browser details with a secret that changes every day. We use it to count unique visitors. We never store the network address itself, and the daily secret is deleted after two days, so older fingerprints cannot be recreated.

When someone reports a link, we store the link, the reason, any details they write, and a scrambled network fingerprint that is deleted within about two days so that repeat reports can be ignored. We also keep short-lived counters, built from a scrambled network identifier, to limit abuse. They are deleted within about two days.

When an administrator acts on a link, report or account, we write it in an audit log so there is a record of what was done and why.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and send you sign-in links, which is the service you asked for.
  • To show link owners their analytics.
  • To keep the service safe: blocking abuse, handling reports and limiting spam. This is a legitimate interest of ours and of our users.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or build advertising profiles.

Cookies

We set one cookie, ph_session, only after you sign in. It keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and the browser hides it from page scripts. There are no tracking or advertising cookies. If you use the light and dark switch, your choice may be remembered in your browser on your own device.

How long we keep it

  • Individual clicks: 90 days. After that only daily totals remain (counts by country, device, browser and referring site, with nothing that identifies a person).
  • Daily totals: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in links: valid for 15 minutes and usable once.
  • Sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Daily fingerprint secrets and abuse counters: about two days.
  • Your account, links and saved QR styles: until you delete them.
  • Reports and the administrator audit log: kept so we can show what was done and why.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database, handles our domain, and runs the Turnstile security check on the sign-in and report pages.
  • Resend delivers our sign-in emails, so it sees your email address and the message.

Both work in several countries, so your data may be processed outside Nigeria, the UK and the EU. We do not use Google analytics or any advertising service.

Your choices

In Settings you can download everything we hold about your account as a file, and you can delete your account. Deleting it removes your email address, sessions, sign-in links, click records, daily totals and saved QR styles. Your short links stay reserved as empty entries with no destination or name, so nobody can take over an address that was printed or shared. If an administrator blocked a link for abuse, its destination is kept as evidence.

You can also ask us to correct or delete data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for visitors in the UK and the EU, the UK and EU GDPR. You may complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Abuse checks

People who run phub.ink may look at link and traffic data when handling a report or investigating abuse.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect. The code is open source, so you can also read exactly what the service does.
