The rules

Terms and acceptable use

Last updated 10 November 2026. By using phub.ink you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

The service

phub.ink is a free service run by PhoniquesHub in Lagos, Nigeria. It makes short links and QR codes and shows analytics for them. You need an account to create tracked links. You keep your account safe by keeping your email inbox safe, because that is how you sign in. You are responsible for what you do with your account and for the links you create.

Acceptable use

Do not use phub.ink to create or share links that lead to:

  • Phishing, scams, fraud, or pages that pretend to be someone else.
  • Malware, harmful downloads, or anything that attacks a device or a website.
  • Spam, or links sent in bulk to people who did not ask for them.
  • Illegal content, including child sexual abuse material, content that incites violence, and anything else that breaks the law in Nigeria or where the content is sent.
  • Content that harasses people, or that exposes private information about them.

Do not try to get around the limits below, probe or attack the service, or use it to hide where a link really goes in order to mislead people.

Reports, removal and suspension

Anyone can report a link on the report page, and a person reviews every report. We may disable or remove any link, block a website address, and suspend or close an account, at any time and without notice, if we think these rules are broken or if the law or a safety concern requires it. We may share information with the authorities where the law requires.

Fair-use limits

There is no monthly cap on links or QR codes. To keep the service healthy for everyone, we apply daily and hourly limits. Today they are:

  • Up to 20 new links a day in an account's first 7 days, then up to 200 a day.
  • Up to 30 new links an hour per account.
  • A limited number of sign-in requests per hour, and a daily limit on sign-in emails sent by the whole service. If it is reached, sign-in may be unavailable until the next day.
  • A limited number of reports per hour from one network.

We may change these limits. The service runs on free infrastructure, so extremely heavy use may also be slowed or stopped by the platform's own limits.

QR codes

A tracked QR code contains a phub.ink short link, so it only works while phub.ink is online and the link is active. If we shut down, or remove the link, it will stop working. A plain code made in the QR studio contains the address, text, Wi-Fi details or email itself, so it keeps working without us. Always test a code on a few phones before you print it.
